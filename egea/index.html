Enumerative Geometry in East Asia
A regional workshop with the world in focus

Institute of Mathematics, Academia Sinica

Taipei, 25-29 December 2023

This workshop aims to bring together researchers in enumerative geometry, very broadly defined, at all levels, from East Asia and beyond. A goal of this first edition is to launch a new partnership in East Asia, but with the world in focus.
