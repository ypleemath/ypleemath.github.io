Information for Students

In any of my (undergraduate) classes, there is usually a warning like the following in the syllabus.

The goal of this class is to have students learn the subject well and then to give them fair and accurate grades. To achieve this goal, this instructor believes in serious homework/quiz problems and hard exams. Serious problems make students learn more and better. Hard exams give a better evaluation of students' learning. In other words, if you are taking this class just to get a grade and with no intention to learn, consider taking another class.
